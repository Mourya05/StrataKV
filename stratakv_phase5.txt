Task 3: Implement include/stratakv/generation/generator.h and src/generation/generator.cpp

This is the core autoregressive loop.

State: Holds the TransformerBlock, BlockPool, PageTable, and Sampler.

Generation Loop: std::vector<int> generate(const std::vector<int>& prompt_tokens, int max_new_tokens).

Execution Flow:

Allocate a sequence in the PageTable.

Feed prompt_tokens through the forward pass.

Enter the while loop: Pass the last hidden state to the language model head to get logits.

Call sampler.sample(logits) to get the next_token.

Append next_token to the sequence via page_table.append_token().

Repeat until max_new_tokens or an EOS (End of Sequence) token is reached.
